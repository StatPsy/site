I am a researcher in cognitive and social psychology with experience conducting experimental research as part of grant-funded research projects. In my previous research, I studied cognitive control, unconscious error detection, and the mechanisms underlying behavioral adaptation following errors, including post-error slowing, error repetition, and the influence of prior experience and situational factors on performance in cognitive tasks.

Since September 2026, I have been working in the StatPsy lab at the University of Lausanne as a PhD student under the supervision of Prof. Audrey Bürki. As part of my doctoral research, I am interested in language production and the cognitive mechanisms underlying it. In particular, I am interested in the influence of age on language production, as well as language production processes under multitasking conditions.
