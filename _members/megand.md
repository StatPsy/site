Hi! I'm Megan Dailey, and I am a postdoctoral researcher in phonetics and psycholinguistics. I joined Dr. Audrey Bürki Foschini’s team in February 2025 on the L2 Spell grant. We are currently investigating how spellings influence the development of second language (L2) speech sound representations and productions. 

I earned my Ph.D. in Cognitive Science from the ENS – Université PSL in Paris, where I investigated bilingual processing of phonetic detail and non-native sociophonetic perception in French at the Laboratoire de Sciences Cognitives et Psycholinguistique. My interest in language and cognition was first nourished at The Ohio State University, before I continued as a Master’s student in the Cognitive Science Master’s program at ENS – PSL. 

I am in awe of our extraordinary propensity for language learning through all stages of life. In my research, I seek to uncover all that we know about the speech sounds of our second+ language – how we perceive and produce them, what individual and cognitive factors lead to their acquisition, to what extent we associate speech sounds with new social meanings, and how all of this knowledge affects language processing in real time.
